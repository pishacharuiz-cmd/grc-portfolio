# Corporate Access Control Policy
**Version:** 1.0  
**Scope:** All employees, contractors, and third-party systems accessing corporate assets.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to establish controls for logical access to information systems and data assets, ensuring protection against unauthorized disclosure, modification, or destruction.

## 2. Authentication Standards
* **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Mandatory for all corporate email, cloud environments, version control platforms (GitHub), and internal administrative tools. Hardware-based tokens or secure authenticator apps are required.
* **Password Complexity:** Passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters, incorporating alphanumeric and special symbols, with automated rotation policies enforced every 90 days.

## 3. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
* Access is granted based on the Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP). Employees are provisioned only the permissions strictly required to execute their specific job functions.
* Access requests must be formally approved by a direct manager or system owner.

## 4. Periodic Review
* Access entitlements for all internal repositories and cloud infrastructures must be reviewed on a quarterly basis by department leads. Orphaned or terminated accounts must be revoked within 24 hours.
