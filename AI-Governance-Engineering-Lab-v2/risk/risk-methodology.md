# AI Risk Methodology

## Purpose

Provide a repeatable portfolio-level method for identifying, scoring, treating, and monitoring AI governance risks.

## Scoring

Likelihood and impact are each scored from 1 to 5.

`Inherent Risk Score = Likelihood x Impact`

| Score | Illustrative rating |
|---:|---|
| 1-4 | Low |
| 5-9 | Moderate |
| 10-16 | High |
| 17-25 | Critical |

## Assessment Workflow

1. Identify the AI system and business purpose.
2. Identify data, users, vendors, model dependencies, and affected stakeholders.
3. Identify governance, privacy, security, fairness, and human-oversight risks.
4. Score likelihood and impact.
5. Assign controls and a risk owner.
6. Define treatment and residual risk.
7. Validate control evidence.
8. Review the risk periodically or after material changes.

These scores are illustrative portfolio assumptions and are not a substitute for an organization's formal risk methodology.
