# ITGC Control Objectives

## ITGC-01 — User Access Management

### Control Objective
Ensure user access to organizational systems is authorized, appropriate for the user's job responsibilities, and periodically reviewed.

### Control Requirement
User access should be approved by an appropriate manager or system owner before access is granted.

### Testing Approach
The auditor will:

1. Review the user access listing.
2. Verify that access is associated with an appropriate role.
3. Verify evidence of management approval.
4. Identify unauthorized or unsupported access.
5. Document exceptions and assess associated risk.

---

## ITGC-02 — Privileged Access Management

### Control Objective
Ensure privileged accounts are appropriately authorized, restricted, and periodically reviewed.

### Control Requirement
Administrative access should be limited to authorized personnel with a documented business need.

### Testing Approach
The auditor will:

1. Review the privileged account listing.
2. Identify users with administrative privileges.
3. Verify documented authorization.
4. Evaluate whether privileged access is appropriate for the user's responsibilities.
5. Document exceptions and assess associated risk.

---

## ITGC-03 — Change Management

### Control Objective
Ensure changes to production systems are authorized, tested, documented, and approved before implementation.

### Control Requirement
Production changes should have documented approval and evidence of testing before deployment.

### Testing Approach
The auditor will:

1. Review a sample of production changes.
2. Verify change requests exist.
3. Verify appropriate approval.
4. Verify testing evidence.
5. Verify implementation documentation.
6. Identify exceptions and assess associated risk.

---

## ITGC-04 — User Termination

### Control Objective
Ensure terminated employees and contractors have their system access removed promptly.

### Control Requirement
System access should be disabled following employee or contractor termination in accordance with organizational policy.

### Testing Approach
The auditor will:

1. Review the terminated-user listing.
2. Compare termination dates with account disablement dates.
3. Identify accounts that remained active after termination.
4. Document exceptions.
5. Assess the associated risk.
6. Recommend remediation where necessary.
